01 ABOUT YUGMA

Smart Preparation.
Stronger Future.

YUGMA is an AI-powered interview preparation and career-readiness platform designed to help students build strong communication, technical, and professional skills. The platform provides a personalized environment where students can practice interviews, improve their confidence, and understand their strengths and weaknesses.

02 HOW YUGMA WORKS
01

Build Your Skill Profile

YUGMA can analyze a student's resume, academic background, projects, technical skills, and previous interview performance to create a personalized preparation experience.

02

Practice AI Interviews

Students can participate in AI-based mock interviews with real-time questions and feedback designed around their individual skills and career goals.

03

Live Camera Experience

YUGMA can provide a live interview experience using camera access, allowing students to practice speaking naturally while improving eye contact, confidence, facial expressions, and body language.

04

Analyze & Improve

After every session, YUGMA can provide performance analysis, identify weak areas, and recommend topics for improvement.
