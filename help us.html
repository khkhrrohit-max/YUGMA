01

What is YUGMA?

Your AI-Powered Career Preparation Platform

YUGMA is an AI-powered platform designed to help students prepare for technical interviews, HR interviews, coding rounds, aptitude tests and career opportunities.

YUGMA creates a personalized preparation experience by using your resume, academic background, technical skills, projects and previous performance.

The goal is simple: Practice → Get Feedback → Improve → Practice Again.

03

How AI Mock Interview Works

1

Create Your Profile

Enter your academic details, technical skills, projects and career interests.

2

Add Your Resume

Upload your resume so YUGMA can understand your education, skills, projects and experience.

3

Select Interview Type

Choose the type of preparation you want, such as technical, HR, behavioral or role-specific.

4

Allow Camera & Microphone

If you want a live interview experience, allow browser access to your camera and microphone.

5

Talk With AI Interviewer

Answer questions naturally as if you were attending a real interview.

6

Get Feedback

Review your answers and identify areas that need more practice and improvement.

7

Practice Again

Work on your weak areas and repeat the interview until you become more prepared.

04

Why Does YUGMA Need Camera & Microphone?

Eye-Gaze Practice

Helps you practice maintaining appropriate visual attention during an interview.

Posture & Position

Helps you become aware of your sitting position, head position and movement during practice.

Voice Practice

Voice analysis can help evaluate speaking speed, pauses, filler words, pronunciation and clarity.

Camera and microphone access is optional and requires your browser permission. YUGMA uses these capabilities to support interview practice and communication analysis.
