OUR PURPOSE

What is YUGMA?

An intelligent platform for career preparation.

YUGMA is designed to provide students with a structured and personalized environment for interview and career preparation.

Instead of preparing only from books or watching interview videos, students can actively practice interviews, answer technical and HR questions, participate in coding practice and receive performance feedback.

YUGMA aims to create a continuous preparation cycle where students practice, understand their weaknesses, improve their skills and attempt another interview.

Our Mission

To make high-quality interview preparation more accessible, interactive and personalized for students.

Personalized preparation AI-based mock interviews Real-time practice Performance analysis

WHAT WE PROVIDE

How YUGMA helps students

AI Mock Interviews

Practice technical, HR, behavioral and role-specific interviews with an AI-powered interview experience.

Live Interview Experience

With permission, students can use camera and microphone access to practice a more realistic interview environment.

Communication Practice

Improve speaking clarity, fluency, pronunciation, speed, pauses and overall communication.

Coding Practice

Prepare for technical interviews with coding questions and practical problem solving.

Performance Analysis

Analyze interview performance and identify areas where further preparation is required.

Student Skill Profile
